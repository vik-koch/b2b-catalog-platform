This page is demonstration text for a fictional shop. A real deployment replaces it with its own terms of sale.

Who we sell to

We sell only to businesses, institutions and public bodies, for use in the course of their work. We do not sell to consumers.

How an order becomes a contract

Sending an order is a request. We check it and confirm it, with the delivery date and its cost, and the contract is made when we do. Where something has to change, such as a quantity we cannot supply from one lot, we agree it with you before confirming.

Prices

The prices shown are those of the moment the order is sent, excluding VAT. Delivery and payment are described on the payment and delivery page.

Returns

Faulty goods are replaced or credited. Sterile goods whose packaging has been opened cannot be taken back.

Claims

A claim reaches us in writing, by email or by letter to the address in our company information, naming the order reference.
