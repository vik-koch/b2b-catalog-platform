Invoices and payment terms

We sell to businesses and institutions only. Prices are shown excluding VAT, which is added at the rate that applies to each product. An invoice is payable by bank transfer within 30 days of its date; universities and hospitals may quote a purchase order number in the order note.

Certificates

Certificates of analysis and sterility are issued per production lot. Where a lot matters to you, say so in the line note and we will supply the whole quantity from one lot.
