From a warehouse beside the River Slaney in Enniscorthy we supply laboratory consumables to research groups, hospitals, food and water testing labs and manufacturers across Ireland.

What we do

We stock the plasticware, glassware and protective equipment a lab goes through every week — pipette tips, tubes, dishes, vials, gloves — and deliver it on trade terms, from a single sleeve of dishes to a standing monthly order for a whole department.

How we work

  • Stock held in Ireland, so most orders leave within a working day
  • Certificates of analysis and data sheets with every product that has them
  • Prices agreed per customer, from academic to contract terms
