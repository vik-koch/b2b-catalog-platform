By ticking the box when you register, or when you first choose your password, you consent to us processing your details to decide on your account and to run it once it is open.

What we process

What you tell us when you register or we open the account for you: your name, email address and phone number, and for a company its registration number. Then the addresses you save and the orders you place.

What for

To assess your registration, to show you your prices, and to let you order and follow your orders. Our privacy policy describes each in detail.

How long

For as long as the account is open. We keep a record that you gave this consent, with the version of this text you saw, for a while after it ends as proof, and then delete it.

Withdrawing it

Withdrawing this consent means deleting your account, which you can do yourself from your account page.
