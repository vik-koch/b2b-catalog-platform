Coffee Kontor — email gallery

Every message the app can send, rendered from this deployment's own wording.

Signing up and getting in

Registration received (to the applicant)

Sent on sign-up. Deliberately actionless: the account cannot sign in until staff approve it.

Subject: We received your registration

New registration (to the shop)

Everything the applicant submitted, for a manager deciding whether they are a customer the shop knows.

Subject: New registration

Invitation — registration approved

The link that sets the first password, for an account staff approved.

Subject: Your account is ready

Invitation — account created by staff

The same link for an account nobody applied for, so it says where the account came from.

Subject: An account has been created for you

The account itself

Password reset

Requested from the login form, or sent by staff on the account holder’s behalf. The link lives an hour.

Subject: Reset your password

Account deleted

Confirms the deletion the customer asked for. Past orders are anonymised, not removed.

Subject: Your account has been deleted
